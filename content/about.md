Teams usually get in touch when firmware, hardware and security have to work
together: a board to bring up, a design to verify, a platform to secure, or engineers
to train.

## What the work covers

- **Firmware and RTOS.** Drivers, board bring-up and debugging on Arm Cortex-M and
  Cortex-A, bare-metal or on an RTOS, and embedded Linux where the product needs it.
- **FPGA and SoC.** RTL design, AXI interconnects and DMA, timing and performance
  work, and verification with SystemVerilog and UVM.
- **Secure design.** Threat modelling and firmware security reviews, TrustZone,
  secure boot chains, root of trust and secure firmware update.
- **Training.** Courses and mentoring for engineering teams on Arm, RTOS, embedded C
  and C++, and HDL.

## Background

Experience ranges from FPGA design for high-speed communication links and SoC design
for a hardware security tool to a wearable medical device taken from a blank page to a
production-ready design. Today the work is split between consulting for engineering
teams and technical training.

Learning continues alongside it. Recent courses cover secured embedded Linux (secure
boot, OP-TEE), Zephyr, RISC-V and Rust.

## Outside work

Four years as a volunteer with the Lebanese Civil Defense, in first aid and emergency
response, and two summers leading youth teams in digital media projects.

Everything on this site is based on public documentation and personal projects.
