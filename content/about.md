I work on embedded systems security across three layers: firmware, digital
hardware and system-on-chip.

## What I work on

- **Firmware and RTOS.** Embedded C and C++ on Arm Cortex-M, real-time operating
  systems, and the security features around them.
- **FPGA and digital design.** HDL design and verification.
- **SoC security.** Evaluating how a system-on-chip holds up against attack.

## Background

- Engineering degree in embedded security and verification, Grenoble INP – ESISAR (2023)
- Engineering degree in electronics and telecommunications, Lebanese University (2019)
- Professional experience in FPGA design, SoC security and technical training
  (Arm, RTOS, embedded C/C++, HDL, hardware security)
- Languages: English, French, Arabic

Everything on this site is based on public documentation and personal projects.
