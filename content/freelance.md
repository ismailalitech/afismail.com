## Problems I can help with

- A board that does not come up, or firmware that crashes and nobody can say why.
- Firmware to write or restructure on Arm Cortex-M, bare-metal or on an RTOS.
- A processor system and FPGA fabric that have to work together.
- HDL that needs a design review or a verification plan.
- A product that needs TrustZone, secure boot, or a plan that shows its protections work.
- C or C++ that needs a review for memory safety.

## What an engagement can include

- A defined piece of work with an agreed result: a driver, a bring-up, a review report.
- Debugging with your team, on your hardware.
- A written review: what I found, how serious it is, and what I recommend.
- A handover: documented code and a walk-through for the people who will maintain it.

## How it starts

1. You send three things: the hardware or platform, the current issue or goal, and the timeline.
2. I reply with questions, or with a short proposal: scope, deliverables and duration.
3. We agree on the scope in writing before any work starts.

## Team training on request

I run hands-on sessions for engineering teams when a team asks for one. Example subjects:

- Zephyr
- Arm Cortex-M and TrustZone
- FPGA and HDL
- Embedded Linux

A session is built around exercises on a board: each participant builds, flashes and
debugs the code, and keeps the material afterwards. The content is prepared for your
hardware and your team's level, so tell me both when you write.
