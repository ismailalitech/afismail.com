Three tasks share one core. The low task makes a call into the Secure world. Watch who waits, then change the service or split the call into pieces.

What it is built for

  1. Measure

    About forty experiments time each source of Secure-side delay on two boards, with hardware timestamps.

    How it is timed

  2. Model

    Each delay becomes a term of a response-time analysis: blocking, interference and a cost per preemption.

    The analysis in ten minutes

  3. Mitigate

    Split long services, keep flash code and handlers in SRAM, restore compiler builtins. Each fix is measured.

    Chunking a service

  4. Enforce

    A cap, a budget and a client identity inside the Secure partition keep one client from taking everyone's deadlines.

    The runaway client

  5. Validate

    The bounds are checked on random task sets nobody designed, on the hardware.

    96 random sets
